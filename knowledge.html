Book 01 · Founders and early stage

The Order of Chaos

A founder's blueprint to scaling businesses.

Most startups do not fail because of a bad idea. They break under the weight of chaotic growth. The problem is not the hustle. It is the architecture.

This book gives founders a master blueprint for building a company that lasts: the Architectural Scaling Framework, a 9-cell matrix that shows you your growth stage and the exact moves it needs. Inside: how to escape product-market fit confusion, remove operational chaos as you scale, build a moat that lasts, and grow from founder to CEO.

Not a book of disconnected advice. A complete, first-principles operating system for turning disorder into order.

Book 02 · Mid-sized business

The Last Firefighter

Merging AI and Six Sigma for predictive quality.

Every quality professional knows the 2 AM phone call. This book is how you stop answering it.

Drawing on work in telecom, banking, healthcare and BPO, HK Borah shows how to stop chasing defects and start predicting them: a practical 90-day framework that merges Six Sigma discipline with AI pattern-finding. Inside: catch billing errors before customers do, cut repeat calls and agent burnout, read hidden patterns in complaints and chats, spot fraud in real time, and build the business case your CFO will approve.

No PhD required. No expensive software. Stop reacting. Start preventing.

Book 03 · Enterprise

VEGA

The Safe Sigma method for governed AI velocity.

More than 80% of AI projects fail. The technology is not the problem. The missing operating discipline is.

VEGA - Velocity, Enforcement, Governance, Assurance - runs on three constructs in a strict sequence. Shield merges governance and cybersecurity into one function. Rails keep AI as a reasoning node, never execution authority. Speed is deployed last, at the point of highest friction, only after Shield and Rails are locked.

Written for CEOs, CFOs, CROs, independent directors, CISOs, CTOs and AI engineers tired of watching prototypes crash. Speed is available to everyone. The Shield and the Rails are not.
