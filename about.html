About Builder, not consultant

I have built, broken, and rebuilt businesses.

That is the difference. I did not study businesses from the outside and then start advising. I ran them, got things wrong, fixed them, and wrote down what actually worked.

The Short Version

What I actually do.

I help owners turn a business that depends on them into a business that runs without them. Founders get their first structure. Mid-sized businesses get their leaks closed and their technology working. Boards get governance that holds when things go wrong.

I do it in three ways. Books, so you can learn the frameworks yourself. A Digital Twin, so you can get a first opinion free, any hour. And execution, when you want someone to do the work with you rather than hand you a report.

My clients are in Canada, the USA, Brazil, India and Singapore. The framework does not change with the time zone.
