Questions

Questions.

Is this really free?

Yes. No signup, no card, no pitch. Ask anything you want. We only ask that you use it honestly.

Is my conversation saved?

No, not by default. Nothing is stored while you chat. If you press Save, you get a PDF on your own device. Nothing is sold or shared, ever.

Is my data used for training?

No. Your conversation is not used to train any public model, and it is not shared with anyone.

What if the twin does not know the answer?

It will tell you. It will not bluff. If your question is outside what it knows, it will say so and suggest you book a call.

Is this legal or professional advice?

No. The twin gives general business advice based on published frameworks. It does not know your specific facts, your legal exposure, or your finances. It is not a substitute for a formal engagement or a licensed professional. If the answer matters, book a requirements call.

Where can I read your frameworks in more depth?

Three books. The Order of Chaos for the founder stage, The Last Firefighter for the mid-sized stage, and VEGA for enterprise governance.
